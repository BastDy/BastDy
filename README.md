#### What changed on client CRMs

Measured at Turnk (2024 to 2026), where I ran HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive for 8 B2B client teams.

| Result | What it means for the team |
|---|---|
| **1 day** lead response time, down from 4 | Demo requests reach the right rep while the buyer still remembers the form |
| **+30%** conversion after lead scoring and routing | Reps spend their time on leads that fit |
| **2 sec** to produce a quote, down from 1 hour | The quote goes out while the buyer is still on the call |
| **-30%** duplicate records | Nobody calls the same account twice, reports stop double counting |
